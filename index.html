About

Hi, I'm Rod — a San Diego–based software engineer currently shifting focus from frontend development into AI engineering: building real applications on top of large language models rather than training models from scratch.

Most recently I spent time at ServiceNow, where I built frontend components for a new internal AI platform using the Lit framework, and led the development of multi-agent AI workflows — including a system that automated defect triage and one that converted 270 manual test cases to automated execution using the Claude API.

That work is what pulled me toward AI engineering full-time. I'm currently going deep on retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases, and agentic tooling, working through an IBM AI Engineer certification, and building projects aimed at practical, unglamorous B2B use cases — the kind of internal tooling that's boring on the surface but genuinely valuable underneath.
